Privacy Policy

Fin Win — Smart Wealth • Effective Date: June 1, 2026
🔒 Zero Data Collection Guarantee: Fin Win is a 100% offline-first application. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal financial records or transaction data on external cloud servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Fin Win operates entirely locally on your device's browser/storage sandbox:

  • We do not require user registration, accounts, or email logins.
  • We do not collect your financial transactions, account balances, merchant names, or budget limits.
  • We do not track your location, IP address, or browsing behavior.

2. Local Storage & Data Security

All transactions, custom budget limits, and user preferences (such as currency selection and accent themes) are saved securely on your device using browser localStorage. You retain full ownership and control over your data at all times.

If you perform a Master Reset within the app settings, or clear your browser application data, all stored records are permanently deleted from your device.

3. Offline PDF Generation & Device Permissions

Fin Win generates financial statements and reports directly inside your device memory. No document data is ever uploaded to remote servers during export.

4. Third-Party Services

Fin Win does not integrate with third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or remote SDKs.
